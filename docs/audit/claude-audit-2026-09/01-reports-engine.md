# Audit 01 — Reports module & reporting engine (14 Sep 2026)

Read-only code audit. Paths relative to repo root.

## Headline findings

1. **Critical — every "canonical NETVALUE" sales figure is ex-GST, and NETAMOUNT is discarded at import.** `src/Etp.Reporting.Import/Profiles/RetailSalesProfiles.cs:65-67` maps NETAMOUNT→source_net_amount, TAX→source_tax_amount, NETVALUE→source_net_value, but `src/Etp.Reporting.Infrastructure.SqlServer/R025SqlImportOrchestrator.cs:54` persists `source_net_value` (NETVALUE) into the sales-line net slot and null into gross. So `dbo.sales_lines.source_net_amount` holds NETVALUE (ex-GST). DSR (`OperationalReportRepository.cs:614-617`), Titan/Helios/Combined/Brand/Segment/Item/Returns (`SqlServerReportingQueryRepository.cs:9-12`), Invoice Summary (`ORR:189`), Staff (`ORR:328`, R013 orchestrator `RetailEnrichmentSqlImportOrchestrator.cs:176`), Management Trend (`Phase2OperationsRepository.cs:403`) and the pack all report ex-GST. `RetailReportingPolicy.cs:8` declares `ApprovedSalesAmountSource.Net`; switching to Gross would block everything because gross is always null. Docs/knowledge asserting "NETVALUE includes GST" (decision DATA-004) are wrong: `docs/05_MAPPING_REGISTER.md:21`, `docs/26_REPORT_CATALOGUE_EXPANSION.md:7`, `docs/PROJECT_CLOSURE_TRACEABILITY.md:103`, `knowledge/02-Business-Domain/Data Dictionary.md:16-17`, `knowledge/05-Mappings/Mapping Knowledge.md:14`.
2. **Critical — DSR "Service WDC" can never be populated.** `ORR:673` reads `field_code IN('SALES_TARGET','SERVICE_WDC')` but SERVICE_WDC is not in `manual_input_definitions` (`database/migrations/0005:24-36`, `0008:4-10`) and the FK (`0005:69`) prevents inserting it.
3. **High — monthly and staff targets effectively unusable.** DSR target read only for `business_date=@date` (`ORR:673`) so the monthly SALES_TARGET must be re-keyed daily. Staff targets need `period_start=@from AND period_end=@to` exact match (`OperationalCompletionRepository.cs:182`).
4. **High — MTD/YTD service and walk-in figures null unless every day keyed.** `ORR:409-410` requires `CurrentCount == days×3`; `ORR:662` requires `COUNT(DISTINCT business_date)==InclusiveDayCount`. With Jul-start data and FY from 1 Apr, YTD service/walk-ins/conversion are structurally impossible.
5. **High — visual Excel/preview KPIs sum non-additive and percentage columns.** `VisualReporting.cs:60-62,85-89` totals the first four numeric columns; DSR export sums FTD+MTD+YTD across stores; Staff sums "Growth %"; `IndianNumberFormatter` (`VisualReporting.cs:41`) formats percent with "0.0%" which multiplies by 100 again (110.2 → "11020.0%"). `ReportPresentationSession.cs:54` composes a VisualReport for every report and `ReportExportCoordinator.cs:117-118` routes Excel to the visual exporter, so every Excel export carries these KPIs.
6. **High — daily pack PDF truncates numbers.** `ReportPackExporters.cs:258-267` equal column widths, clip at columnWidth/4.8 chars; `Escape` (`:283`) replaces every non-ASCII char with `?` (₹, —). Same in `SimplePdfReportExporter.cs:68-89,98`.

## B. Gap analysis vs the owner's six reports

Brand rows (NEBULA/EDGE/XYLYS/AUTOMATIC/RAGA; SEIKO/CITIZEN/CERRUTI) absent from DSR, no brand master (High). Walk-ins/conversion null unless every day entered (High; `ORR:641-666`). WCC WALKIN/SALES, WDC BILLS absent (High). Combined rows OK.

**B2 Closing stock by brand:** `stock-physical` has the right columns but groups by `COALESCE(cluster, brand_name, brand_code, product_code)` — cluster first (`ORR:461,465`), so brand-level counts impossible when clusters exist (High). `stock-brand` is system-only (High gap). Physical treated as separate counted entry not Display+Backstock+Defective+YLoc (Medium). FAIL on any variance, no tolerance (`ORR:491`).

**B3 Cash book:** only CASH tender pulled (`ORR:434`); Card/UPI/CN/TC/service tenders and total sale absent (High). No carry-forward of previous closing to opening (High; `ORR:430-437`). Single store/date one row. Blocks on non-required CASH_ADJUSTMENT/CLOSING_CASH_COUNTED (`CashBalanceReconciliationService.cs:31-33` vs `0008:8-9`) (Medium).

**B4 Service:** manual inputs only; WDC impossible; YTD/LY structurally null (High); DSR service total = SumIfAny shows partial as complete (Medium; `DailySalesReportDocument.cs:114`).

**B5 Customer-wise invoice list:** not available; CUSTOMERNAME never mapped (`RetailSalesProfiles.cs:56-77`, policy `ReportSourceRegistry.cs:20`); grand total (NETAMOUNT) not stored (High/Critical).

**B6 Staff/CRO:** ex-GST (Critical); exact-period target lookup (High); FAIL on paisa mismatch (Medium; `ORR:381-382`); contribution 0 instead of N/A (Low).
