Formula Help

How it works: Set up staff in Employee Master, enter days in Attendance, review computed pay, approve & lock, then export Salary Slips.

  • Extra Days: Deduction ≤5.5 → +4 days; 6–6.5 → +2 days; ≥7 → 0 days.
  • OT Days: Days earned beyond the monthly cap, paid as overtime.
  • PF/ESIC: Per-employee toggles in Employee Master. Rates configurable in Settings.
  • Professional Tax: Maharashtra slabs by gender & February — see/edit in Settings.
  • Single firm: This build is dedicated to Saagar Traders only.
