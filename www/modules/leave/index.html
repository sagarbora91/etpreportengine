Weekly Off (non-trading days)
Leave dates on these days are auto-skipped. Uncheck both to record leave every day of a 7-day trading week (jewellery / watch stores). Default: Sun + Sat.
Per-Store Leave Capacity (max staff off per day)
Advisory only — flags a store as over-capacity in the day view. Does not block adding leave. Staff store comes from the central Employee Master.
Peak-Season Blackouts & Store Holidays
Blackout days warn against new leave; tick Block to hard-stop it. Store Holiday just marks the day. Store scope uses the central Employee Master to decide who is affected.
